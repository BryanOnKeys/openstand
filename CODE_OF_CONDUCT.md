# Code of conduct

OpenStand is made by musicians and developers of every level, from people writing their first pull request to professional players. We want it to be a place where all of them feel welcome.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to code or to music.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. "The bass misses the approach in bar 4" helps; "this is amateur" does not.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- Accept that musical taste differs. Disagree with the groove, not the player.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminating remarks of any kind
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission
- Repeatedly derailing discussions, or trolling

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer, @canbmaj7, privately: open an issue that says only that you'd like a private contact, and you will get one, or use GitHub's **Report content** option. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and may block people who do not follow this code.

This code is based on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
